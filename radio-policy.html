Overview & Purpose

Falcon Radio is an internet radio streaming platform operated by Falcon, a Sri Lanka-based media and education company. This Content & Copyright Policy governs all audio content broadcast through the Falcon Radio platform, including music, spoken word, sound effects, jingles, and any other audio material.

The purpose of this policy is to ensure that all content broadcast on Falcon Radio complies with applicable copyright law, respects the intellectual property rights of original creators, and maintains a safe, lawful, and high-quality listening environment for all users.

By using Falcon Radio as a broadcaster or listener, you agree to the terms set out in this policy. This document should be read alongside Falcon's general Terms of Service.

Third-Party Content: Any third-party content broadcast through Falcon Radio must be properly licensed by the broadcaster prior to use. Falcon does not grant licences for third-party content and is not responsible for unlicensed use by broadcasters.

Important: When you stream content through Falcon Radio, you represent and warrant that you have all necessary rights, licences, consents, and permissions to broadcast that content to a public audience over the internet.
03

Copyright & Licensed Music

Music copyright is one of the most important areas of compliance for any internet radio broadcaster. Playing a commercially released song without the proper licence is a copyright infringement, regardless of whether the broadcast is free or paid.

Performing Rights Organisations (PROs): In most countries, internet radio stations are required to obtain licences from performing rights organisations such as ASCAP, BMI, SESAC (United States), PRS for Music (United Kingdom), or the relevant PRO in Sri Lanka. These licences cover the right to publicly perform musical compositions.

Master Recording Rights: In addition to composition rights, broadcasters must also obtain master recording licences from record labels or distributors when streaming commercially released recordings.

Royalty-Free & Creative Commons Music: Broadcasters may freely use music that is explicitly licensed under Creative Commons (CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA, etc.) or released as royalty-free, provided the licence terms are followed — such as crediting the artist when required.

Recommendation: If you are unsure whether your music is properly licensed, we strongly recommend using royalty-free music libraries such as Epidemic Sound, Artlist, Free Music Archive, or YouTube Audio Library for your broadcasts.

Falcon Radio uses Zeno.fm for stream delivery. Broadcasters are solely responsible for ensuring their content complies with all applicable licensing requirements. Falcon accepts no liability for unlicensed music broadcast by third-party station operators.

Prohibited Content

The following content is strictly prohibited on Falcon Radio. Violation of these rules may result in immediate suspension of broadcasting privileges:

  • ✗ Copyrighted music, recordings, or audio broadcast without a valid licence
  • ✗ Content that incites violence, hatred, or discrimination based on race, religion, gender, nationality, or sexual orientation
  • ✗ Defamatory, slanderous, or knowingly false statements about individuals or organisations
  • ✗ Sexually explicit or adult-only content broadcast on unlabelled or general-audience streams
  • ✗ Content that promotes or facilitates illegal activity
  • ✗ Personal information of individuals shared without their consent (doxxing)
  • ✗ Impersonation of Falcon, other broadcasters, public figures, or organisations
  • ✗ Spam, repetitive filler content, or automated broadcasts intended to game platform metrics
  • ✗ Content that violates the laws of Sri Lanka or the broadcaster's country of residence

DMCA & Takedown Procedure

Falcon respects the intellectual property rights of all creators and complies with the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA) and equivalent international copyright frameworks.

Filing a Takedown Notice: If you believe that content broadcast through Falcon Radio infringes your copyright, please send a written notice to falcon.incoroporated@gmail.com with the subject line "Copyright Takedown Notice". Your notice must include:

  • → Your full name and contact information
  • → A description of the copyrighted work you claim has been infringed
  • → The specific stream or broadcast where the infringing content appeared
  • → A statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is not authorised
  • → A statement that the information in the notice is accurate, under penalty of perjury
  • → Your physical or electronic signature

Falcon will investigate all valid notices and take appropriate action, which may include removing access to the infringing content and notifying the broadcaster. Repeat infringers will have their broadcasting privileges permanently revoked.

Counter-Notices: If you are a broadcaster who believes your content was removed as a result of a mistake or misidentification, you may file a counter-notice by contacting us at the same email address with appropriate supporting documentation.
